Ghana Rent Act (Act 220) — Section 17 Disclosure

Section 17 of the Rent Act, 1963 (Act 220) makes it a criminal offence for a landlord to remove a tenant, change locks, remove belongings, cut utilities, or otherwise force a tenant out without a lawful order. This notice does not, by itself, authorise your landlord to remove you or re-enter the property. Only a Rent Magistrate or court can order recovery of the property, following a referral to the Rent Control Department.
